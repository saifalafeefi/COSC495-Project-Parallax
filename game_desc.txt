---

PROJECT TITLE
Carbon Conquest XR

Platform
Mobile AR (Unity LTS 6000.3.9f1 + AR Foundation + URP)

Game Type
Single-Player Sustainability Strategy
Tabletop AR Experience

Core Vision
The player places a large holographic low-poly Earth (0.6m–1.2m diameter) onto a real-world surface. The Earth is fully interactive. The player walks around it physically or rotates it via touch gestures. Each region of the Earth can be tapped to inspect and modify environmental policies.

The objective is to prevent global climate collapse over 8 rounds by strategically managing carbon emissions, economic growth, and public stability across world regions.

---

CORE EXPERIENCE FLOW

1. Player scans horizontal surface.
2. Player taps to place Earth sphere (anchored AR object).
3. Earth appears at ~0.8m default diameter.
4. Player can:

   * Physically walk around Earth.
   * Rotate Earth via drag gesture.
   * Adjust scale via UI slider (clamped range 0.4m–1.2m).
5. Player taps a region on the Earth.
6. Region data panel appears.
7. Player selects and applies a sustainability policy.
8. Visual changes occur on that region.
9. Repeat for 3 actions per round.
10. After 8 rounds → Final score evaluation.

---

EARTH MODEL & REGION STRUCTURE

Earth Representation

* Single low-poly sphere mesh.
* Stylized continents.
* Subdivided into 8–12 logical regions.

Regions are NOT separate meshes unless needed. Regions are defined logically using latitude/longitude mapping.

Region Data Model (C# class)

Each Region contains:

* string RegionName
* float CarbonLevel (0–100)
* float EconomyLevel (0–100)
* float StabilityLevel (0–100)

Initial Values

* Carbon: 50
* Economy: 50
* Stability: 50

---

REGION SELECTION LOGIC

On touch input:

1. Raycast from AR camera through screen touch.
2. Detect collision with Earth collider.
3. Convert hit point into local space of Earth.
4. Normalize vector and compute:

   * Latitude
   * Longitude
5. Map lat/long to predefined region index.
6. Highlight selected region.

Highlight Behavior:

* Subtle glow overlay.
* Slight scale pulse.
* Optional outline shader.

---

REGION VISUAL FEEDBACK

Visual state dynamically reflects CarbonLevel:

Carbon < 30

* Lush green tint.
* More trees visible.
* Clear atmosphere shader.

Carbon 30–70

* Neutral balanced visuals.

Carbon 70–85

* Brown tint.
* Light smoke particles.
* Slight dimming.

Carbon > 85

* Fire or flood particle effects.
* Red glow.
* Reduced city brightness.

Economy affects:

* Density of city lights.
* Number of buildings visible.

Stability affects:

* Subtle UI indicators (warning icon if low).

ROUND SYSTEM

Total Rounds: 8
Actions Per Round: 3

After each round:

For each region:

* If Carbon < 30 → Carbon –2 passive recovery
* If Carbon > 70 → Stability –2 penalty
* If Carbon > 85 → Crisis Trigger

Crisis Trigger:

* Economy –10
* Stability –10
* Persistent particle effect until Carbon < 80

---

GLOBAL SYSTEM

GlobalCarbon = Average(CarbonLevel of all regions)

If GlobalCarbon > 75:

* Board tint shifts toward red.
* Ambient storm sound increases.

If GlobalCarbon > 85:

* Immediate Game Over.
* Message: “Global Climate Tipping Point Reached.”

---

SCORING SYSTEM

After Round 8:

FinalScore =
(HealthyRegions × 20)

* (AverageStability × 0.5)
* (AverageEconomy × 0.5)
  – (GlobalCarbon × 1.5)

Victory Ratings:
90+ → Sustainable Future
70–89 → Stable Transition
50–69 → Fragile Balance
Below 50 → Environmental Failure

---

XR INTERACTION DESIGN

Earth Controls:

* Drag horizontally → rotate Earth around Y axis.
* Drag vertically → slight tilt (clamped to prevent inversion).
* Scale slider adjusts EarthRoot.localScale.
* Earth remains anchored to AR plane.

Region Inspection:

On tap:

* World-space panel appears above selected region.
* Panel shows:
  Region Name
  Carbon %
  Economy %
  Stability %
* “Apply Policy” button opens bottom policy panel.

UI Layout:

Top Floating Panel:

* Round Counter
* Global Carbon
* Average Economy
* Average Stability

Bottom Policy Panel:

* 3–6 policy cards
* Tap to apply to selected region.

---

AUDIO DESIGN

Ambient:

* Light wind + subtle world hum.

High Carbon:

* Storm ambience.
* Distant thunder.

Policy Application:

* Clean energy → uplifting tone.
* Industrial → heavy mechanical tone.
* Crisis → warning alarm cue.

---

SCOPE CONTROL NOTES

Do NOT:

* Add real-time spreading simulation.
* Add complex weather systems.
* Add dozens of regions.
* Add procedural generation.

Keep:

* 8–12 regions maximum.
* Simple integer math.
* Clear visual states.

---

PROJECT SUMMARY

Carbon Conquest XR is a single-player AR tabletop sustainability strategy game where the player interacts with a large holographic Earth placed in real space. The player taps regions, deploys policies, manages carbon levels, and prevents global climate collapse across 8 rounds through strategic decision-making.

The experience emphasizes:

* Meaningful XR spatial interaction.
* Clear sustainability mechanics.
* Visual environmental feedback.
* Strategic trade-offs.
* Controlled technical scope.
